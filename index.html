ABOUT
SHAWN

Shawn Perera is an emerging pop and alternative artist born in Sri Lanka and currently based in Melbourne, Australia.

His artistic direction explores the space between traditional Sri Lankan influences and contemporary pop and alternative sounds.

Through music, SHAWN aims to connect cultures, experiences and emotions while developing a sound that is uniquely his own.
